# AgriGuard - 3 Minute Live Demo Pitch Script

**(Slide 1: Problem)**
"Good afternoon, judges. Look at these farms. Across Africa, extreme weather is devastating smallholder farmers. When disaster strikes, traditional insurance is useless. Why? Because manual damage verification takes 21 days or more. It's expensive for insurers and often too late for farmers, leading to bankruptcy. The system is broken."

**(Slide 2: Solution)**
"Meet AgriGuard. We are reducing the manual verification bottleneck with a real-time parametric insurance platform that turns geospatial evidence into auditable claims."

**(Slide 3: Technology/Engine)**
"How do we reduce routine field checks? We feed geospatial evidence into our rules engine. We visualize live GEOGLOWS ECMWF streamflow forecasts alongside VIIRS thermal hotspots; in this demo, the claim trigger uses clearly labeled simulated EO metrics through the same rules engine. Crucially, we cross-reference those signals with GNSS-captured WGS84 farm boundaries and their device/accuracy metadata. The result is an auditable, data-driven trigger."

**(Slide 4: Live Demo - Transition to Dashboard)**
"Let’s see it in action. Here on our dashboard, you see a registered maize farm mapped with a GNSS polygon. Our Parametric Engine is constantly monitoring this exact area.
*(Trigger the flood mock script in the background now)*
Let's simulate a severe weather event through the same rules engine used for a production feed. The live GEOGLOWS and VIIRS layers remain visualization context, while this demo uses clearly labeled simulated trigger metrics.
*(Wait 2 seconds for dashboard to update and flash red)*
Boom. The system just detected that water levels exceeded our critical 2.5-meter threshold for over 3 days within the farm's precise boundaries. 
Notice what just happened:
1. The alert was generated instantly.
2. The rule engine created a claim after reaching a 95% multi-source confidence score.
3. *(Hold up phone to microphone/camera)* And the farmer just received an SMS status alert on their offline phone. Because Web3 is not configured in this demo, the claim is transparently marked PENDING rather than showing a fake paid transaction."

**(Slide 5: Business Model)**
"This is a B2B platform for insurance companies. Reducing routine field verification targets a 90% operational-cost reduction. For the farmer, our target is to move claim processing from weeks to minutes."

**(Slide 6: Conclusion)**
"We are AgriGuard. With GNSS-defined farm evidence, live geospatial context, and a configurable settlement path, we are working toward faster climate resilience for African farmers. Thank you."
